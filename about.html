01 / THE CREATOR

ABOUT ME

I'm just a gamer and a Resident Evil fan who wanted to make a cool fan-made project for these lovely characters who have made this franchise even more special to me.

02 / INSPIRATION

WHAT INSPIRED THIS SITE

One of the biggest inspirations behind this website was @DEXBTHO on YouTube and their website. Seeing their work inspired me to create something of my own, with my own style and focus.

My other inspiration is simply my love for these lovely characters. I wanted to create a place where I could appreciate and showcase them in a way that feels personal to me.

03 / THE PROJECT

BIOHAZARD: HEROINES

Biohazard: Heroines is an unofficial Resident Evil fan archive dedicated to the series' female protagonists and heroines.

The website features pictures, wallpapers, model viewers and other visual content collected and organized as a personal fan project.

04 / NOTE

A LITTLE NOTE

This website was made out of appreciation for the characters and the Resident Evil series. It was also made with the help of AI (ChatGPT) during the creation process. It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.

Thank you for visiting Biohazard: Heroines.
